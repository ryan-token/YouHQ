# YouHQ

Your life, organized.

YouHQ is a personal command center for life's important details. Track everything from home maintenance and vehicles to career history and where all of your money is, all in one place.

The app is built with [Swift](https://www.swift.org), [SwiftUI](https://developer.apple.com/swiftui/), and [SQLiteData](https://github.com/pointfreeco/sqlite-data). It works across Apple Platforms, seamlessly syncs between devices with CloudKit, and even supports sharing with others over iCloud.

## Features

**Residences**:
- Support for multiple residences
- Keep track of utilities, insurance policies, maintenance items, paint colors, and more
- Get notifications when it's time to take care of a maintenance item
- Monthly TCO (total cost of ownership) calculator

**Vehicles**:
- Support for multiple vehicles
- Keep track of insurance policies, maintenance items, paint colors, and more
- Get notifications when it's time to take care of a maintenance item
- Monthly TCO (total cost of ownership) calculator

**Finances**:
- Keep track of where all of your money is
- Log your bank accounts, investment accounts, retirement accounts, HSAs, other insurance policies, and more so you don't lose track of your money

**Media**:
- Keep track of all of your devices (brands, models, purchase dates, etc)
- Keep track of all your service providers, like your TV service and cell carrier, and any of your subscriptions

**Career**:
- Log your jobs and career moves over time
- Track your salary changes over time

## Security & Privacy

- I don't store ***any*** of your data
- All data stays local to your devices and is synced securely over iCloud
- Seriously, I don't even run servers for this app
- I use [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com) for very basic app analytics, and I don't store any private or personally identifiable info there

## Other
