# YouHQ

YouHQ is a personal life management app built with [Swift](https://www.swift.org), [SwiftUI](https://developer.apple.com/swiftui/), and [SQLiteData](https://github.com/pointfreeco/sqlite-data). It works across Apple platforms, seamlessly syncs between devices with CloudKit, and even supports sharing with others over iCloud.

## Features

**Residences**:
- Support for multiple residences
- Keep track of utilities, insurance policies, maintenance items, paint colors, and more
- Get notifications when it's time to take care of a maintenance item
- Monthly TCO (total cost of ownership) calculator

**Vehicles**:
- Support for multiple vehicles
- Keep track of insurance policies, maintenance items, paint colors, and more
- Get notifications when it's time to take care of a maintenance item
- Monthly TCO (total cost of ownership) calculator

**Finances**:
- Keep track of where all of your money is
- Log your bank accounts, investment accounts, retirement accounts, HSAs, and more so you don't lose track of your money

**Media**:
- Keep track of all of your devices (brands, models, purchase dates, etc)
- Keep track of all your core subscriptions (TV service, cell carrier, etc)

**Career**:
- Log your jobs and career moves over time
- Track your salary changes over time

## Security & Privacy

- I don't store ***any*** of your data on my servers
- All data stays local to your devices and is synced securely over iCloud
- Seriously, I don't even run servers for this app
- I use [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com) for very basic app analytics, and I don't store any private or personally identifiable info there
